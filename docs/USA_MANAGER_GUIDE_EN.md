# One Market USA — Super Admin Guide

The primary USA operator is a **One Market Super Admin**. He has the same operational management powers inside One Market as the Super Admin / Owner, while his day-to-day responsibility is the US market.

Target profile:

```text
role = super_admin
primary_market = US
```

`primary_market = US` is an operational focus, not a permission restriction.

Main responsibilities include US sellers, catalog quality, orders, customer support, delivery partners, payment operations, marketplace performance and US growth.

NKS Services remains the owner of the One Market product, brand, root infrastructure/accounts and the main source-code repository.

Critical changes involving authentication, RLS, payments, permissions or destructive database operations should be cross-reviewed by both Super Admins.
